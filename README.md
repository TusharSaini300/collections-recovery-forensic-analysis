## Central conclusion
The reported `~11% month-on-month` number is arithmetically reproducible as **Feb→Mar gross recovery: ₹17.01 Cr → ₹18.89 Cr = +11.03%**. However, the supplied brief does not disclose the exact leadership numerator, denominator or attribution rule, and corrected recovery/account also produces an ~11% Feb→Mar movement.

That single favorable month pair is not evidence of sustained improvement. Across complete Jan–Jul months, gross recovery has no significant linear trend (p≈0.89). After correcting the point-in-time working population, aligning numerator and denominator, standardizing January DPD × risk × geography mix, and tracking the fixed January cohort, the like-for-like recovery signal remains effectively flat.

## Investment decision
**HOLD ₹10 Cr** pending a controlled targeting experiment with a pre-registered incremental recovery KPI and intervention-specific cost inputs.
