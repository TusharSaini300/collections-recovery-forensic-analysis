# P3 — Reconciliation Bridge: Reported 11% vs. Like-for-Like Recovery

## Executive result

The reported `~11% month-on-month recovery improvement` is **arithmetically reproducible for the February → March gross-recovery pair**, but the exact leadership KPI definition is not disclosed.

- February gross recovery: **₹17.01 Cr**
- March gross recovery: **₹18.89 Cr**
- Feb → Mar gross-recovery change: **+11.03%**
- Feb → Mar corrected recovery/account change: **+11.73%**

Because two plausible KPI definitions produce an ~11% increase over the same pair, the exact numerator/denominator/attribution rule behind the leadership claim remains **UNVERIFIED**.

The full-series evidence does not support sustained improvement. Gross recovery has no significant Jan–Jul linear trend (p≈0.89), while population-aligned, mix-standardized and fixed-January-cohort measures remain effectively flat.

## Bridge

| Step | Definition | Result | Classification |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1. Reported claim | “Recovery improved ~11% MoM” | Basis not disclosed | UNVERIFIED |
| 2. Direct arithmetic bridge | Gross recovery Feb → Mar | ₹17.01 Cr → ₹18.89 Cr = **+11.03%** | FACT |
| 3. Alternative KPI bridge | Corrected recovery/account Feb → Mar | ₹5,618 → ₹6,277 = **+11.73%** | FACT |
| 4. Full gross series | Successful deduplicated recovery Jan–Jul | No significant linear trend, p≈0.89 | STRONG EVIDENCE |
| 5. Population correction | Latest status as of month-start | Jul ratio falls from buggy ~₹12,680 to corrected ₹9,532 | FACT |
| 6. Numerator/denominator alignment | Recovery only from month-start working accounts | Jan ₹6,241/account → Jul ₹6,230/account = **-0.2%** | STRONG EVIDENCE |
| 7. Mix standardization | January DPD × risk × geography mix | Jan → Jul ≈ **0.0%** | STRONG EVIDENCE |
| 8. Fixed opening cohort | Same January working accounts tracked through July | Jan → Jul ≈ **+0.01%** | STRONG EVIDENCE |
| 9. Investment implication | Historical observational evidence | Do not commit ₹10 Cr without controlled lift/cost evidence | DECISION |

## Interpretation

The correct message is not that the Feb→Mar +11.03% movement did not happen. It did.

The correct message is:

> **The reported ~11% can be reproduced as a single favorable month-pair, but it is not evidence of sustained system-level improvement. Once the metric is evaluated over the full Jan–Jul period and tested with population alignment, mix standardization and a fixed January cohort, the underlying recovery signal is effectively flat.**

The dataset therefore does not support using the reported ~11% as the basis for a ₹10 Cr investment decision.
