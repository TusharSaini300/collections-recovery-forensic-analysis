## 3. Population / denominator issue

The corrected month-start working population falls:

30,000 → 26,838 → 24,667 → 22,852 → 21,444 → 20,364 → 19,643.

**Jan ₹6,241/account → Jul ₹6,230/account = -0.2%.**

By July, ~34.6% of gross recovery came from accounts outside the month-start working population.

**Jan ₹6,241/account → Jul ₹6,241/account ≈ 0.0%.**

**Jan ₹6,241/account → Jul ₹6,241/account ≈ +0.01%.**

## 5. Do not label this automatically as Simpson's paradox

The evidence demonstrates a population/denominator artifact and numerator/denominator mismatch. It does not require a Simpson's-paradox label. The safer classification is **denominator/population artifact**.
